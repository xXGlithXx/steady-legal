Community Guidelines

Last updated: 15 August 2026

Steady is for people trying to train and focus consistently. The social side exists to make that easier — not to build an audience, and not to compete. These rules are short because the bar is simple: don't make this a worse place for someone else to show up.

What Steady is for

Sharing your own genuine training and focus. Encouraging people you follow. Small accountability groups. That's it. There's no follower count to chase, no leaderboard, and no reason to perform.

What isn't allowed

There is zero tolerance for objectionable content and abusive behaviour. Accounts that post it have the content removed and may be suspended or deleted without warning.

Toward other people

  • Harassment — targeting, pile-ons, unwanted contact after being blocked, or comments designed to make someone feel bad about their body or their progress
  • Hate — anything attacking people for who they are: race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, disability, sex, gender identity, or sexual orientation
  • Threats or violence, including anything glorifying it
  • Impersonation — pretending to be someone else, or implying an association you don't have
  • Sharing someone else's private information

In what you post

  • Sexually explicit material, or nudity beyond ordinary gym clothing
  • Anything illegal, including promoting the sale of controlled or prescription-only substances
  • Content someone else owns — post your own photos
  • Spam — advertising, recruitment, links out, or anything unrelated to sharing your own activity
  • Anything involving a minor in a sexual or exploitative way. This is reported to the authorities, not just removed.

Around health

Fitness apps attract this, so it's worth being explicit. Don't post content promoting eating disorders, extreme restriction, or dangerous weight-loss practices, and don't give medical advice. Steady is not a medical service — see the Terms of Use.

If you're struggling with food, exercise or your body, please talk to a doctor or a support line in your country. This app is not the right tool, and neither is the comment section.

What we do about it

Automatic screening

Captions, comments and shared notes are checked automatically as you write them. Text that breaks these rules is refused before it posts — nothing is published and nothing is sent. You'll see an explanation and your words are kept so you can edit them.

Reports

Every post, comment and profile has a ••• menu with Report. Reports are read by a person, not a machine, and are reviewed within 24 hours.

Anyone can also report content from the web, without a Steady account.

Blocking

You can block anyone from their profile. It takes effect immediately: you stop seeing each other, and they can't follow you again. You never need a reason and they aren't told.

If we remove something of yours

You'll be told — what was removed, why, whether an automated filter or a person made the decision, and how to challenge it. Open Settings → Notices in the app. A person reads every challenge, and if we got it wrong we'll say so and put it back.

What happens to accounts that break the rules

It depends on what happened. A one-off may just mean the content goes. A pattern of it, or anything in the zero-tolerance list above, means the account goes. Serious illegal content is reported to the relevant authorities.

Age

You must be at least 13 to use Steady. If you're below the age of digital consent where you live, you also need a parent or guardian's permission. If you believe a child is using Steady, tell us and we'll remove the account.
