Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 August 2026 — App version 1.0

The short version: Steady requires an account and stores your data on servers we control (hosted by Supabase in the EU). Your workouts, focus sessions and profile sync to that account so they survive changing phones. Anything you post to the feed — captions, photos, comments — is visible to people who follow you, and your name, handle, bio, photo and post counts are visible to any signed-in Steady user. Steady has no advertising, no analytics SDK, no tracking across other apps, and never sells your data.

1. Who we are

Steady ("the App", "we", "us") is developed by Avesta Alamgiralam ("the Developer"), who is the data controller for the purposes of the UK GDPR and EU GDPR. This Privacy Policy explains what information Steady collects, why, who can see it, and the choices you have. It applies to the Steady iOS application and its widget/Live Activity extension.

Contact: avestaalam@gmail.com

2. Data we collect

2.1 Account

An account is required to use Steady. You can create one with an email address and password, or with Sign in with Apple. If you use Sign in with Apple and choose to hide your email, we only ever receive Apple's relay address. Passwords are hashed by our authentication provider — we never see or store the password itself.

2.2 Profile

Your display name, unique handle, bio and profile photo. Signing up asks for your name and nothing else.

Steady does not collect your biological sex, date of birth, height or weight. Earlier versions asked for these to compute a readiness and body-composition estimate. That feature was removed, and as of 13 August 2026 so were the fields: the app no longer asks for them, no longer stores them, and any values previously held for existing accounts have been erased from our database.

2.3 Training data

Workout sessions (exercises, sets, weights, duration), saved routines, and focus sessions and categories. This syncs to your account so it survives reinstalling the app or moving to a new phone.

2.4 Social content

Posts you choose to share (captions, photos, workout and focus summaries), comments, reactions, who you follow and who follows you, crews you join, and messages you send in a crew. Users you block, and reports you file.

2.5 Approximate location (optional)

If you grant location permission, Steady rounds your position to roughly one kilometre on your device before sending it, and stores only that coarse figure. It is used solely to suggest nearby Steady members you might know. It is never shown to other users, never returned by any part of the app that displays profiles, and is only ever read inside the suggestion calculation. Decline the permission and everything else works unchanged.

2.6 Notifications

If you enable push notifications, we store an Apple-issued device token so we can send you alerts about follows, comments and reactions. It identifies the device installation, not you personally, and is deleted when you sign out or disable notifications.

2.7 Apple Health

Steady only writes to Apple Health — it reads nothing. When you finish a workout, Steady saves it to Apple Health so it appears in the Fitness app and counts toward your rings. It requests write access only. Earlier versions of Steady read heart-rate variability, sleep and step data to compute a readiness score; that feature has been removed and the app no longer requests or receives any health data. As required by Apple, we confirm that Steady does not use HealthKit data for advertising, marketing or use-based data mining, and does not sell it to anyone.

2.8 What we do not collect

  • No advertising identifiers, no ad networks, no cross-app or cross-site tracking.
  • No third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDK. Crash logs stay on your device and are only shared if you choose to send them.
  • No contacts, no photo library beyond images you deliberately attach to a post, no microphone, no precise GPS coordinates.
  • No body metrics — no biological sex, date of birth, height or weight.

4. Where your data is stored

Steady uses Supabase for its database, authentication and file storage, hosted in the EU (eu-central-1, Frankfurt). Photos live in Supabase Storage: post photos are in a private bucket reachable only through short-lived signed links, while profile pictures are in a public bucket, since they are shown throughout the app.

6. Legal basis for processing (GDPR)

  • Performance of a contract — running your account and syncing your training data.
  • Consent — approximate location, push notifications, and writing workouts to Apple Health. Each is optional and can be withdrawn in iOS Settings at any time.
  • Legitimate interests — keeping the service secure and free of abuse (rate limiting, blocks, reports).

7. How long we keep it

Your data is kept while your account exists. Deleting your account removes your profile, posts, comments, reactions, follows, crew memberships, training data, uploaded photos and the reports you filed. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

Two things intentionally outlive an individual record. Reports made about an account are kept after that account is gone, so that repeated abuse can still be recognised and acted on; they identify the reported content, not the reporter. Anti-abuse throttling records hold only a user id, an action name and a timestamp, and are discarded as soon as they fall outside the throttling window.

8. Deleting your account

In the app: Settings → Account → Hard Reset — Delete Account. This deletes the account itself and everything tied to it, on the device and on the server. No email to us is required. You can also write to the address in section 1 and we will action it.

Note that Apple does not permit any app to reset its own Health permissions. To revoke Steady's write access to Apple Health, use iOS Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Steady.

9. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct, export, delete or restrict processing of your data, and to object to it. Most of these you can exercise directly in the app — Settings lets you edit your profile, export your workout and focus data, and delete your account outright. For anything else, email the address in section 1; we will respond within 30 days. You also have the right to complain to your national data protection authority.

10. Children

Steady is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

11. Security

Traffic is encrypted in transit with TLS. Session tokens are held in the iOS Keychain, and app files use iOS complete file protection, meaning they are unreadable while the device is locked. Server-side, every table enforces row-level security so one account cannot read another's data. No system is perfectly secure, but we do not store payment details and we never see your password.

12. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially we will update the date at the top and, where the change is significant, notify you in the app. Continuing to use Steady after a change means you accept the updated policy.
