Report Content

Last updated: 15 August 2026

Anyone can use this page. You do not need a Steady account to report something, and you do not need to have seen it inside the app. Email avestaalam@gmail.com with the details below and we will look at it.
If someone is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services first. We are a small service and cannot respond as quickly as they can.

Reporting from inside the app

If you have an account, this is faster and gives us more context automatically. Every post, comment and profile has a ••• menu with Report. You can also block someone from their profile, which immediately stops you seeing each other and prevents them following you again.

Reporting illegal content

This is the route required under Article 16 of the EU Digital Services Act, and it is open to everybody. Send an email with as much of the following as you can:

  • Where it is — the username of the person who posted it, and roughly when it was posted. A screenshot is ideal.
  • What is wrong with it — explain why you believe it is illegal, and under which country's law if you know.
  • Your name and an email address we can reply to. You may report anonymously, but we then cannot tell you the outcome, and anonymous reports about alleged offences involving a named individual are harder for us to act on.
  • A statement that you believe, in good faith, that the information in your report is accurate and complete.

Put Illegal content in the subject line so it isn't missed among support mail.

What happens next

  • We confirm we've received it, to the address you gave us.
  • We review it — normally within 24 hours.
  • If we remove something, the person who posted it is told what was removed, why, whether an automated tool was involved, and how to challenge it. They see this inside the app under Settings → Notices.
  • We tell you what we decided and why, where you've given us a way to reply.

Reporting a copyright or trademark problem

If something on Steady uses work you own, email the same address with Copyright in the subject and include:

  • What the work is, and evidence that you own it or act for the owner
  • Where the infringing content is on Steady
  • Your contact details
  • A statement that you believe in good faith the use isn't authorised
  • A statement that the information is accurate, and that you are the rights holder or authorised to act for them

If we remove content on this basis, the person who posted it can challenge the decision, and we may restore it if their challenge holds up.

Challenging a decision we made

If we removed something of yours and you think we got it wrong, open Settings → Notices in the app. Each notice explains the decision and has an option to contest it. A person reads every challenge.

If you disagree with the outcome, you can take the matter to a court, or to an out-of-court dispute settlement body certified under the Digital Services Act in your country. Using this page does not affect those rights.

How we moderate

Two things happen. Captions, comments and notes are checked automatically as they're written, and text that breaks the Terms of Use is refused before it is posted — nothing is published and nothing is sent. Separately, reports from people are read and decided by a person, not a machine.

Where an automated tool was involved in a decision, we say so on the notice the user receives.

Contact point
