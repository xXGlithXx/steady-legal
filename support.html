Steady — Support

Last updated: 13 August 2026 — App version 1.0

Need help? Email avestaalam@gmail.com. Steady is built and maintained by one person, so replies usually arrive within two to three days. Including your iOS version and what you were doing when the problem happened makes a fix much faster.

What Steady is

Steady is a training and focus tracker with a small social layer. You log workouts and focus sessions, build routines, and share what you did with the people who follow you. There is no advertising and no third-party analytics — see the Privacy Policy for exactly what is stored and who can see it.

Common questions

Where do I find these links in the app?

Settings → About holds the Privacy Policy, Terms of Use and this support page. Settings → Account holds sign-out and account deletion. Settings → Data & Privacy holds import, export and clearing data.

Do I need an account?

Yes. Steady syncs your training data to an account so it survives reinstalling the app or moving to a new phone, and the social features need one. You can sign up with an email address and password or with Sign in with Apple.

I forgot my password

On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot password and enter your email. A reset link is sent to that address. If you created your account with Sign in with Apple there is no password to reset — use the Sign in with Apple button instead.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Settings → Account → Danger Zone → Hard Reset — Delete Account. This permanently deletes your account and everything attached to it — workouts, focus sessions, routines, profile, posts, comments, reactions and uploaded photos. It cannot be undone and there is no recovery window. If you cannot reach the app, email the address above from the account's email and the deletion will be done manually.

How do I get a copy of my data?

Settings → Data & Privacy → Export All Data writes your workouts, focus sessions and routines to a file you can save or share. The same screen can also clear your workouts or focus sessions individually without deleting your account.

Someone posted something abusive

Every post, comment and profile has a report option behind its ••• menu, and you can block a user from their profile. Blocking immediately removes them from your feed and prevents further contact. Reports are reviewed and acted on within 24 hours — accounts that post abusive or objectionable content are removed. You can also email the address above.

My workouts aren't syncing between devices

Sync runs whenever the app is open and online. Check that you are signed in to the same account on both devices (Settings → Account shows the account email), and that the device has a working connection. Data is stored locally first, so nothing is lost while offline — it uploads on the next launch with a connection.

Steady isn't saving workouts to Apple Health

Steady writes finished workouts to Apple Health and reads nothing from it. If workouts aren't appearing, open the iOS Health app → Sharing → Apps → Steady and confirm write access is enabled.

Notifications aren't arriving

Check iOS Settings → Steady → Notifications is enabled. Steady only sends notifications about your own activity — new followers, comments and reactions on your posts. There are no marketing notifications.

Reporting a bug or requesting a feature

Email avestaalam@gmail.com. For bugs, the most useful things to include are:

  • What you expected to happen, and what happened instead
  • The screen you were on
  • Your iPhone model and iOS version
  • A screenshot or screen recording, if you have one
